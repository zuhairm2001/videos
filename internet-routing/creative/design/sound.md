# Sonic Direction

## Music
- Mood: bright, curious lo-fi chiptune / city-pop-tinged — early-2000s Japanese web, not aggressive synthwave.
- Tempo: ~110–120 BPM, 4/4. Key: F major / D minor feel.
- Instruments (Tone.js): square/pulse lead, soft FM bells, sine sub-bass, bit-crushed drum kit, light vinyl/tape noise bed.
- Structure follows beats: sparse intro (hook) → groove enters at packets → peak at routing → drop to near-silence at arrival → resolve on return.
- Mix under voice: duck ≥8 dB during narration (≥10 dB when the lead melody plays); no melodic lead under stressed words. Master ≈ −14 LUFS, ≤ −1 dBTP.

## SFX palette
| Event | Sound |
|---|---|
| Keystroke / Enter | Short mechanical click, slightly bit-crushed |
| Scramble-resolve text | Fast tick burst (like a dot-matrix printer) |
| Packet hop | Tiny pitched blip, rising pitch per hop |
| Router arrival | Soft modem-ish chirp |
| Glitch transition | 50–100 ms digital crunch |
| Page loaded | Warm two-note chime |

## Voice
- ElevenLabs "Brittney" (`jB2lPb5DhAX6l1TLkKXy`); **smooth but upbeat** — warm, even timbre with bright delivery; clear diction, smiling tone, never shouty or hype-y; 155–165 wpm. Start settings: stability 0.45–0.55, similarity ≈0.75, style 0, speed 1.00–1.05.
- Script drives timing via `script/script.json`.
